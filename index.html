💨 InSight Martian Wind Rumble

On Dec. 1, 2018, InSight's air pressure sensor and seismometer recorded a haunting 10-15 mph low-frequency rumble caused by Martian wind vibrating its solar panels.

⚡ Apollo 12 Artificial Moonquake

On Nov. 20, 1969, Apollo 12's jettisoned ascent stage was steered to crash into the Moon 60 km from the newly deployed ALSEP seismic station, ringing the Moon like a bell!

Mars Pathfinder (1997)

Airbag Descent & 15-Bounce Landing Simulator

Altimeter: 330 m Bounces: 0 / 15

In 1997, Mars Pathfinder pioneered a beach-ball landing: 24 interconnected airbags inflated 100 meters above Mars, bouncing 15 times up to 50 feet high across Ares Vallis before rolling to a stop.

Apollo 15 (1971)

Galileo's Equivalence Principle: Hammer vs Feather Drop

Commander David Scott's Live Experiment

On August 2, 1971, Commander David Scott dropped a 1.32 kg geological hammer and a 0.03 kg falcon feather in front of the TV camera on the Moon to prove Galileo's law in a true vacuum.

Lunar Vacuum (1.62 m/s²): No air molecules to cause drag. Ready for drop.
Pioneer 10 (1972)

Interstellar Plaque Cryptography Explorer

Destination: Red Star Aldebaran

In case Pioneer 10 is intercepted by intelligent extraterrestrial life, it carries a gold-anodized aluminum plaque designed by Carl Sagan and Frank Drake to pinpoint Earth's place in the Milky Way.
